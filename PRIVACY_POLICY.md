# Privacy Policy — Stopwatch Baseball

**Last updated: October 18, 2026**

Stopwatch Baseball is an offline baseball game. This policy describes the information handled by the Android app, package name `com.stopwatchbaseball`.

## Information the app stores

The app stores game information on your device so that an unfinished game can be resumed. This can include player and team names that you enter, game settings, scores, and current game progress. The app stores this information in its private app storage.

The app does not require an account and does not ask for your contacts, location, photos, or other device data. The current app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, does not collect information for the developer, and does not send game information to the developer or to an app-operated server. The current release manifest does not request Internet access.

Please avoid entering sensitive or identifying information in player or team names; names are visible to people using the device.

## Device backup and deletion

Android system backup may include app data, depending on your device, operating-system version, account, and backup settings. Backups are handled by Android and the backup provider under their own terms and privacy policies.

Stopwatch Baseball clears its saved game data when a game is completed. You can also remove locally stored app data using Android's app-storage settings or by uninstalling the app. Data already copied to a device or system backup may need to be removed through that backup provider.

## Sharing and third parties

The app does not share game data with the developer or other third parties. Android and your device manufacturer may process technical information as part of operating, securing, backing up, or diagnosing your device and apps; that processing is controlled by their respective policies.

This policy page may be hosted by GitHub Pages. Visiting the policy website is separate from using the app, and GitHub may process website request data under [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

## Children's privacy

The app does not ask users to provide personal information or create accounts. The app stores names entered for gameplay locally on the device as described above. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of names and other text entered into the app.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated to describe the new practices. The latest version will be published at this page. The date at the top indicates when it was last updated.

## Contact

For questions or privacy requests concerning Stopwatch Baseball, use the developer contact details shown on the app's Google Play Store listing.
